State Progression System

Each tier should feel like a real step in a financial climb.

Low tiers carry tension. Mid tiers settle into momentum. Late tiers get steadier, cleaner, closer to zero. The thin bars here show illustrative position inside each stage — your live dashboard leads with dollars to the next payoff stage.

Pressure

Tiers 1 to 3

Slouched, unstable, and cramped. Motion stays slightly uneasy so the state feels heavy.

Momentum

Tiers 4 to 7

Posture rises, silhouettes clean up, and the motion becomes controlled. Progress starts to feel durable.

Reward

Tiers 8 to 10

The last stages feel cleaner and more controlled because the target is zero debt, not luxury.
